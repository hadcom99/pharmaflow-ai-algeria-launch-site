Platform capabilities

Six precision levers. One compliant outcome.

Every feature is designed around the specific regulatory and forecasting constraints of Algerian pharmaceutical distribution — not adapted from a generic playbook.

Enforce nine constraints precisely

All nine Algerian regulatory rules — including the 30-day stock floor, controlled-substance allocation ceilings, and GS1 serialization fields — are hard solver constraints. Reconstruct demand before shortages

Detect compliance violations instantly

A real-time constraint monitor runs continuously against live inventory and order state, flagging any position approaching a regulatory threshold before shipment confirmation. Named violations are logged with timestamp, responsible party, and applicable regulation reference.

P50 MAPE is calculated and displayed per SKU across your top-200 lines, giving operations directors a measurable accuracy baseline to present to CFOs and board-level stakeholders. Pilot cohort median MAPE reached 11.3% within the first 60 days of data ingestion.

Methodology

Four steps from raw ERP data to compliant allocation

1

Ingest historical order lines

PharmaFlow connects to your ERP or order management system and ingests every inbound order line — including unserved and partially served quantities that generic platforms discard — capturing the true unconstrained demand signal.

2

Encode regulatory constraints

All nine Algerian regulatory rules are compiled into the solver as hard boundary conditions specific to your licence class and product portfolio. No optimization run can silently relax a constraint — violations block execution and generate a named compliance event.

3

Generate Hijri-aware forecasts

The forecasting engine overlays Hijri calendar features — Ramadan onset, Aïd El-Fitr, and Aïd El-Adha — onto every SKU's demand model, automatically adjusting for the 11-day annual drift as the forecast horizon advances.

4

Execute compliant allocation plans

The constraint-satisfying solver produces allocation and replenishment plans guaranteed regulation-compliant at the moment of generation. Each plan line is traceable to the demand signal, binding constraints, and forecast confidence interval.

About PharmaFlow AI

Built specifically for Algerian pharmaceutical distribution — not adapted from elsewhere

PharmaFlow AI was founded by a team of operations researchers, pharmacists, and regulatory specialists who identified a consistent pattern across Algerian pharmaceutical distributors: the tools that existed were designed for European or North American supply chains and retrofitted with PDF-based compliance checklists.
